FAQs

Common Questions

How much of my donation goes directly to programs? +

92 cents of every dollar donated goes directly to field programs. Our operational costs are independently audited and published annually in our Impact Report.

Do I need special qualifications to volunteer? +

No. We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds. The most important qualities are compassion, adaptability, and respect for local cultures. Specific skills are a bonus, not a requirement.

Can corporations or institutions partner with HopeForward? +

Absolutely. We welcome CSR partnerships, grant funding, in-kind support, and employee volunteer programs. Email partners@hopeforward.org to discuss tailored partnership structures.

Are donations tax-deductible? +

Yes, in most countries where we operate. We are registered as a tax-exempt charitable organization in 18 jurisdictions. A receipt is automatically issued for all online donations.
