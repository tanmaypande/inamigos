Who We Are

A Movement Born from Necessity

HopeForward began in 2008 when founder Dr. Ananya Rao witnessed the devastating impact of preventable disease in rural communities during a field study in Central Africa. What started as a single mobile clinic has grown into an international organization operating in 48 countries.

We are not a charity that hands out aid — we are a development organization that builds capability. Every program is designed with local communities at the center, ensuring cultural sensitivity, long-term ownership, and measurable, sustainable outcomes.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These principles are not aspirations — they are the non-negotiable standards by which we measure every decision, program, and partnership.

🔍

Transparency

Every rupee and dollar donated is tracked and reported publicly. Our annual impact reports are independently audited and freely available.

🤝

Community-Led

Solutions come from within communities, not imposed from outside. We listen first, and design only with the people we serve.

📊

Evidence-Based

We invest only in programs with proven methodologies and rigorously measure outcomes to continually improve our impact.

🌿

Sustainability

Every program is designed to continue without our presence — building local capacity so communities thrive independently.

⚖️

Equity & Inclusion

We actively work to dismantle systemic barriers, prioritizing those most overlooked: women, girls, the disabled, and marginalized groups.

🛡️

Accountability

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards in operations, partnerships, and governance — no exceptions, no compromises.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2008

Founded in Nairobi

Dr. Ananya Rao launches the first mobile health clinic serving 3 villages in rural Kenya with a team of 8 volunteers.

2012

Education Program Launches

First school construction project completed. Curriculum partnerships established with UNESCO and local governments in 6 countries.

2016

UN Recognition & Global Expansion

Awarded Special Consultative Status with ECOSOC. Operations expand to South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Latin America.

2020

COVID-19 Emergency Response

Pivoted all operations to pandemic relief — distributing 2M+ masks, 500k food kits, and establishing 40 telehealth stations.

2024

2.4 Million Lives Impacted

Reached our most significant milestone, now operating in 48 countries with 12,500 volunteers and 340 schools built.
